What is childhood cancer

Unlike adult cancer, childhood cancer needs a different basis for treatment

In cancer treatment today, most physicians assess, treat and prescribe according to cancer clinical practice guidelines. Unlike adult cancer, childhood cancer differs in the intensity and methods of treatment, the risks and outcomes of treatment, and the tolerance of side effects of chemotherapy drugs. Around 80% of pediatric cancer cases in high-income countries can be cured; with active treatment, the condition of most children can be controlled.

Precision medicine is being used to select cancer treatments, including the cultivation of circulating tumor cells (CTCs) for drug testing, aiming to reduce the chances of ineffective treatment. For the related technology, see CancerFree Biotech's EVA Select.

Free Testing Services

For children aged 0–12 with cancer, CancerFree Biotech offers the first round of testing services free of charge

You can discuss this with your primary care physician or contact us via the provided phone number for consultation. The testing process is as follows:

1

Talk with your attending physician

Discuss with your attending physician whether the test is suitable, or call our contact number directly for a consultation.

2

Obtain circulating tumor cells

Circulating tumor cells (CTCs) in the blood are used for the subsequent culture.

3

Biomimetic tumor organ culture

The cells are cultured in a biomimetic tumor organ culture system.

4

Drug testing

The drugs selected by the physician are tested, to reduce the chance of ineffective medication.

Health education on cancer types, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and immunotherapy

Why health education matters

The importance of health education lies in giving people the knowledge and skills they need to make healthy lifestyle choices, prevent disease, improve their quality of life, and deal effectively with health problems.

  1. Disease prevention: provides information on how to prevent infectious diseases, chronic diseases and other health problems, reducing the risk of disease.
  2. Improving health awareness: increases understanding of one's own health, including the ability to detect and deal with disease early.
  3. Promoting healthy lifestyles: encourages a balanced diet, moderate exercise, stress reduction and good sleep habits.
  4. Making healthcare more effective: helps people better understand their medical options and take a more active part in treatment decisions.
  5. Improving community health: encourages community members to take more part in prevention and health promotion activities, lowering health risks for the whole community.

Circulating tumor cells, disease progression, and survival in metastatic breast cancer

The number of circulating tumor cells before treatment is an independent predictor of progression-free survival and overall survival in patients with metastatic breast cancer.

Liquid biopsy and therapeutic response: Circulating tumor cell cultures for evaluation of anticancer treatment

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for the Little Star free test?

For children aged 0–12 with cancer, CancerFree Biotech offers the first round of testing services free of charge. You can discuss this with your primary care physician or contact us via the provided phone number for consultation.

How is the test carried out?

Circulating tumor cells (CTCs) in the blood are cultured in a biomimetic tumor organ culture system, and the drugs selected by the physician are tested, to reduce the chance of ineffective medication.

Why does childhood cancer need precision medicine to help choose medication?

Unlike adult cancer, childhood cancer differs in the intensity and methods of treatment, the risks and outcomes of treatment, and the tolerance of side effects of chemotherapy drugs, so precision medicine can help in choosing cancer medication.
